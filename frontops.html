The workflow

One workflow, from survey to pharmacy order.

FrontOps sits in front of the operation and systems your clinic already uses.

1

Survey

The patient completes a standard intake survey about goals, history and preferences in their own portal.

Step 01
2

Review

The provider reviews the survey alongside any lab results the clinic has.

Step 02
3

Plan

FrontOps aligns patient goals to options from the clinic's own catalog, with the reason for each. The provider changes, removes or adds to them.

Step 03
4

Approve

The provider discusses the plan with the patient and approves it. Nothing moves forward without that approval.

Step 04
5

Order

The approved plan becomes a pharmacy order. Patient charges and pharmacy payables are kept as separate records.

Step 05
The modules

Ordering, Telehealth and CRM.

Ordering

Provider-led care planning and clinic follow-through: survey, provider review, plan and pharmacy order.

Telehealth

Scheduling and remote visits connected to the same patient plan.

CRM

Finding, reaching and following up with the people your clinic serves.

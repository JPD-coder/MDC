The challenge

A single practice can only scale so far on its own.

01

Limited revenue ceiling

Demand for labs, prescriptions, hormone therapy, and weight-management programs continues to grow — services many practices cannot yet offer compliantly.

02

Regulatory complexity

Adding medical services independently requires a medical director, and navigating scope of practice, pharmacy, and laboratory logistics.

03

No defined exit

Independent practices often lack a clear acquirer, a defensible valuation, and a structured path to a competitive multiple.

04

Financial arbitrage

A unified group of independent practices with combined financials and standard procedures commands a substantially higher multiple in a group exit.
